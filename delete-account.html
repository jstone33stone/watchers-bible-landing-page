Delete your account

Watchers Bible · built by Apostolic Intelligence

You can delete your Watchers Bible account and everything stored for it in the cloud at any time, from the app or without it. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

Delete from the app (fastest)

  1. Open the app and go to Settings.
  2. Scroll to Backup & sync.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

The account, its sign-in sessions and every synced row are removed from our servers immediately, in a single transaction.

Delete without the app

If you no longer have the app installed, send an email to apostolic.intel@gmail.com with the subject Delete my account. Include the email address of the account, or the Google address it was created with if you signed in with Google. We verify the request by hand, delete the account and all cloud data within 7 days of verification, and confirm by reply.

What is deleted

  • Your account record: email address, display name and sign-in credentials.
  • Everything synced to the cloud: highlights, notes, bookmarks and reading positions.
  • All sign-in sessions and tokens, the Google sign-in link, and any pending verification or password-reset tokens.

What stays

  • Everything on your device. Deleting the account does not touch the Bibles, highlights, notes or bookmarks stored locally on your phone or computer; the cloud simply forgets you.
  • Anonymous usage statistics and crash reports, if you had them on. These carry no account, email or reading content and cannot be traced back to any account, so deletion does not apply to them.
  • Donation records, which live in the developer's private ledger, separate from accounts. Receipt images are deleted after a manual check. To have your details removed from the ledger or your name taken off the supporter list, email us and we will do it by hand.

Retention

Deletion from the app is immediate. Email requests are completed within 7 days of verification. There is no additional retention period on account or sync data: nothing is copied or archived before the delete.
