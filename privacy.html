Privacy policy

Effective date: November 5, 2026

Watchers Bible ("the app", "we") is a Bible reading app built to be private by default. Your Bibles, highlights, notes and bookmarks live on your device. This page explains exactly what the app stores, what it optionally shares, and what it never does.

Contact: jstone33.jacob@gmail.com

The short version

  • Your reading is yours. Bibles, highlights, notes, bookmarks and reading positions are stored on your device and are never read by us.
  • Cloud sync is optional. Nothing leaves your device unless you sign in and turn sync on.
  • Usage statistics are anonymous and optional. They contain no account, no email and no reading content, and you can turn them off anytime in the app.
  • No ads. No tracking across other apps or websites. No selling of data, ever.

What the app stores on your device

The app works fully offline. The following data lives only on your device:

  • Downloaded Bible translations and the bundled KJV.
  • Highlights, notes and bookmarks you create.
  • Your reading position, preferences and appearance settings.
  • A random, meaningless identifier used only for anonymous statistics (see below).

Uninstalling the app removes this data from your device. Deleting the app's downloaded translations via Settings, Data & storage does not touch your highlights, notes or bookmarks.

Optional cloud sync

If you choose to sign in (with Google or email) and use cloud sync, your highlights, notes, bookmarks and reading positions are stored on our backend (hosted on Vercel and Neon, database and authentication managed by Neon in the AWS US region) so they can follow you between your devices.

We store the minimum needed for sync: your account identifier, your display name and email address from the sign-in provider (so your account can be listed in Settings), and the sync content listed above. We do not read the content of your notes beyond what is technically required to sync them.

You can delete your account and all synced data at any time from the app (Settings, Backup & sync), which removes your account and every synced row from our servers immediately.

Optional anonymous usage statistics

To understand how the app is used and what to improve, the app can send anonymous usage statistics to PostHog (PostHog Inc., cloud-hosted in the United States). This feature is controlled by the "Share anonymous usage statistics" toggle in Settings, Data & storage.

What is collected while the toggle is on:

  • A random install identifier that is not connected to your account, email or identity in any way.
  • The app version and platform (Android, iOS or web).
  • Anonymous events such as: app opens, that a chapter was opened (which translation and Bible book, not what you did with it), that a translation was downloaded, that a verse image was saved, that a daily verse notification or widget was tapped, and that a sign-in was completed.
  • An approximate country, derived from IP address by the analytics provider, to know which languages matter. Full IP addresses are not stored in analytics events.

What is never collected:

  • Your name, email or account identifier.
  • The content of your notes, highlights or bookmarks.
  • The specific verses you read or saved.
  • Advertising identifiers, contacts, photos, location or anything else on your device.

Turning the toggle off stops all collection immediately and erases anything already queued on your device.

Bible downloads

Downloaded translations stream from our storage host (Cloudflare R2). These downloads are anonymous file transfers and carry no information about you beyond what any HTTPS connection technically involves.

Notifications

If you enable it, the daily verse notification and home screen widget are generated on your device from the bundled Bible text. They involve no network connection and no data collection.

Children

Watchers Bible is a general-audience app and does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Accounts are only created by a deliberate sign-in, and the data collected is described above.

Your rights

You control your data:

  • Local data: stored on your device; uninstall or use Settings, Data & storage to manage it.
  • Synced data: delete your account and all cloud data from Settings, Backup & sync.
  • Statistics: turn off the toggle in Settings, Data & storage; the identifier is random and cannot be traced back to you, so no other deletion is possible or needed.

If you have a privacy question or request, contact us at jstone33.jacob@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Material changes will also be announced in the app.
